Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about Quizify

How does the adaptive quiz system work?

Our adaptive system uses machine learning algorithms to analyze your performance in real-time. As you answer questions, the system adjusts the difficulty and focus areas based on your strengths and weaknesses. This ensures that you're always challenged at the optimal level for your current knowledge, making your learning experience more efficient and effective.

Can I create my own custom quizzes?

Yes! With our Pro and Enterprise plans, you can create fully customized quizzes with various question types, including multiple choice, short answer, matching, and more. You can also import questions from existing materials and customize the adaptive settings to match your specific learning or teaching goals.

How can I track my progress over time?

Quizify provides comprehensive analytics and progress tracking features. You can view detailed reports showing your performance trends, knowledge gaps, improvement areas, and learning recommendations. The dashboard visualizes your progress with interactive charts and graphs, making it easy to see how you're improving over time.

Is Quizify suitable for classroom use?

Absolutely! Many educators use Quizify in classroom settings. Our Enterprise plan includes features specifically designed for educational institutions, such as classroom management tools, student performance analytics, and curriculum alignment features. Teachers can monitor student progress individually and as a group, identifying areas where additional instruction may be needed.

What subjects and grade levels are covered?

Quizify covers a wide range of subjects including mathematics, science, language arts, social studies, and more. Our content is available for various grade levels, from elementary school through college. Each subject contains numerous topics and subtopics, ensuring comprehensive coverage of most standard curricula.
